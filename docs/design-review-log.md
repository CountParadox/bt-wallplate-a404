# A404 PoE carrier review

Revision 0.4 draft. KiCad ERC reported 0 errors and 0 warnings.

- **A404 footprint.** The supplied manual screenshots show the J4 and J5 top-view pad order and 28.40 × 29.00 mm board outline. Verify pad pitch and coordinates on the module before making a combined J4/J5 footprint. J5 in the schematic represents the second pad row on the same A404.
- **Bluetooth audio.** Use the original ESP-WROOM-32 MH-ET LIVE ESP32 MiniKit with A2DP sink firmware. The draft uses a project-local 40-pad footprint from https://github.com/r0oland/ESP32_mini_KiCad_Library. Verify the exact board, header geometry, antenna clearance and USB access before layout.
- **I²S.** A404 J5.9 SCLK drives GPIO18, J5.10 LRCLK drives GPIO19, and GPIO23 drives J5.5 SDIN0. J5.3 and J5.12 share GND_ISO. J5.11 MCLK is an A404 output and remains open for the three-wire interface. Confirm A404 stereo I²S mode, slot width and order, and the target left-to-channel-1 and right-to-channel-2 mapping. The manual lists 24-bit PCM at 48/96 kHz. Firmware must bridge the decoded Bluetooth audio rate to the A404 clock, including resampling if the rates differ.
- **Power.** A404 remains powered through J4. J5.13 is open. JP1 feeds the U3 3V3 header pin from 3V3_ISO. Fit the shunt for PoE and remove it before connecting USB. Verify A404 supply tolerance and startup/idle current against the RT9400 V4.3 3.1–3.5 V output range and 200 mA minimum load. Confirm capacitor ESR and ripple requirements and budget the ESP32 load if adding PoE power for U3.
- **Ethernet.** Identify the PHY and check onboard termination and bias. Resolve T1 PHY-side centre taps, RJ45 shield bonding, surge/ESD and isolation spacing. Confirm endpoint MDI pair mapping and switch interoperability.

Intentional opens include unused U3 and A404 pads, RT9400 ADJ, both PHY-side centre taps and the RJ45 shield. Bluetooth reception and network audio await firmware and hardware verification.


Revision 0.6 front controls: GPIO25 drives D1 through 330 ohms. GPIO26 pairs via an active-low button and the firmware pull-up. The 2 x 2 mm WS2812B-2020-V6 uses the MiniKit 3.3 V node and requires at least 3.3 V. RT9400 tolerance extends to 3.1 V, so the LED supply needs margin verified or corrected before release. Confirm actuator travel and LED light pipe against the actual plate. ERC: zero violations.

Partial PCB routing: A404-C2000-fit contains the rear MiniKit and routed front RGB, pairing switch, and associated MiniKit supply/return. It uses the provisional 56 x 80 mm outline. PCB DRC applies only to the populated subset. Remaining board routing depends on measured A404 J4/J5 footprint, RT9400 package revision, confirmed jack land pattern, PHY tap/termination and isolation design, and wallplate depth and mounting geometry.

Revision 0.7 assigns U2 the user-supplied 51.1 mm RT9400 decal. Pad centres from VA1 are 0, 2.5, 5, 7.5, 43.1, 45.6 and 48.1 mm. The left pitch is inferred from the drawing proportions and should be checked on the physical module. Drill is 1.0 mm per decal. Confirm drawing view and actual module lead size. The decal outline projects 7.5 mm on one side of the pin row and 12.5 mm on the other. The PCB fit study's narrow edge-on reserve is superseded by this decal and needs re-placement before routing. ERC: zero violations.

Revision 0.8 assigns separate A404 J4 and J5 pad-row footprints at a shared top-view origin. Use the same origin, rotation and board side when placing them. The supplied drawing establishes 2.00 mm pitch, 26.00 mm row spacing, and first-pad Y offsets of 1.21 mm for J4 and 1.71 mm for J5. The 0.9 mm drill and 1.5 mm pad are provisional for through-hole mating hardware; verify the intended header/attachment before fabrication. Keep the combined 28.40 x 29.00 mm body clear on the module side.

A404 trial placement and I2S routing: rear J4/J5 footprint pair placed together at (131.0, 145.5) mm. The front switch moved left to clear the through-hole row. BCLK, LRCLK, SDOUT and the A404 returns are routed. KiCad board DRC reported zero violations and zero unconnected items for this placed subset. U1 3.3 V and MDI have only one placed endpoint each because the RT9400, magnetics and jack are still pending rear placement. The result is not a full schematic-to-board sign-off.

Revision 0.9 adds the rear Pulse H2019NLT footprint. Four A404 PHY traces remain open in PCB DRC. J1 and U2 are still rear placement reservations. JLCPCB labels the Ckmtw jack Non-PoE, so confirm a suitable shielded top-entry connector before committing J1. The RT9400 decal is 51.1 mm long with up to 20 mm depth across its pin row, so the prior narrow reserve cannot represent it.

Revision 0.10 selects Würth 634108185321 for J1. The manufacturer lists its WR-MJ SMT family as PoE++ compatible and this model is vertical shielded 8P8C. JLCPCB C31498169. Use the exact manufacturer land pattern before routing; CAD ZIP access was denied. The 14.9 x 16.2 mm body fits the previous 17 x 21 mm rear reservation in plan. Plugged cable depth remains to be measured.

Rev 0.11 PCB trial includes rear J1 and U2 and front T1 near J4. J1 land pattern cross-checked against TI TIDA-010083 J2. Right-side outline extends 4 mm above the original trial to clear the RT9400 and preserve the screw-centre area. Front-side T1 requires a faceplate standoff check. A404 PHY traces and RT9400 return are routed. Remaining airwires include cable-side Ethernet and PoE feeds and 3.3 V. Confirm PHY bias, controlled impedance and PoE isolation before fabrication.

Centered layout variant: A404-C2000-centered.kicad_pcb uses a 60 x 88 mm unstepped outline with 3 mm radius corners. J1 is on the rear at the centre of the PCB; D1 and SW1 are on the plate centreline. Rear GND_ISO pour is limited to the isolated side. Retained PHY routing; moved parts need rerouting. Confirm plate ribs, mounting bosses and USB plug/cable overhang on the rotated MiniKit.

Revision 0.12 prototype draft (4-layer). The board was rebuilt from the schematic netlist into dt-wp.kicad_pcb. A404-C2000-centered.kicad_pcb is kept unchanged as the trial reference. C1 and TP1/TP2 footprints are assigned. ERC: 0. Schematic parity: 0. DRC: 0 errors, 0 unconnected, 9 silkscreen warnings. All 21 named nets match the spec cross-check. Stackup is JLC04161H-7628, with a solid GND_ISO plane on In1. PHY pairs are 0.2/0.2 coupled on F.Cu, estimated ~100-108 ohm (not field-solved). A custom DRC rule keeps 2.0 mm in-plane PoE isolation on all layers between cable-side copper (J1 incl. shield, T1 pins 9-16, U2 pins 1-4, MDI/PoE traces) and GND_ISO-side copper; it was verified to flag a deliberate violation. Placement changes: A404 down 3 mm (J1 shield to J5 pin 1 was 0.56 mm), MiniKit up 1 mm, T1 to front right of centre, C1/JP1/TPs rear left. Open blockers: D1 3.3 V minimum vs RT9400 3.1 V; the top fixing-screw axis lands under the MiniKit; module footprints unmeasured; J1 PoE rating; T1 PHY centre taps; faceplate heights. See release-r0.12/README.md.
